About Me Section

Introduction

Hey there! I’m Rohit Shah, a 20-year-old undergrad studying Electrical Engineering (Digital Track) in the U.S. Originally from Nepal, I’ve always been curious about how things work — especially when it comes to tech. While my major covers the hardware side of things, my main focus is on coding and software development. I enjoy building projects that bring together logic, creativity, and real-world problem solving. Whether it’s diving into a new programming language, working on side projects, or exploring the intersection of hardware and software, I’m always looking to learn and level up. Currently, I'm exploring areas like embedded systems, web development, and a bit of AI. Let’s build something cool.

Hobbies

Outside of academics and tech, I’m all about creative expression and personal growth. I love playing the guitar and singing — it’s one of my favorite ways to relax and connect with emotion. Writing poetry is another passion of mine, and I often find myself scribbling down thoughts or verses when inspiration strikes. You can check out some of my written pieces on my poetry blog. I’m also really into Linux and the open-source ecosystem. From exploring different distros to tweaking setups and running everything through the terminal, it’s become a big part of my workflow. In fact, most of my coding projects are Linux-based — I even write about some of my favorite tools and setups on my blog. When I’m not in front of a screen, you’ll probably find me working out — it helps me stay sharp and grounded. Whether it’s music, writing, or hacking away at a terminal, I’m always chasing that sweet spot between creativity and curiosity.

Roah OS

A distro script for Debian & Arch based linux systems.

In search of minimal & resource efficient desktop environment i stumbled upon tiling desktop managers and eventually fell in love with them. This distro (still in progress) brings my configurations that is set up in such a way that it offers seemless coding/programming session. This is recommended for people who want clutterless programming experience and those who have old hardware and want something light for their systems.

For developers & linux experts:Github

For Newbies & normal people:Github

Hyprland

Configurations for hyprland based on arch linux.

A hyprland, a wayland based tiling manager, configuration using hyprland, waybar, tofi, iwd, alacritty and bash scripts.

For more information:Github

Wifi script

A interactice terminal tool for iwd.

A CLI/Terminal based app for iwd, default wifi tool in arch linux. It's a easier, faster and more reliable than traditional network manager. This script was originally written for Roah OS but can be used for any arch based system.
